# HueCorn 🌈🦄

Bring colour back to the world, one crystal at a time!

Guide your unicorn through seven platforming levels, collecting rainbow crystals to unlock new mechanics. Each colour changes the world around you: blocks appear or disappear, platforms start moving, and portals open new paths.

Double jump across gaps, wall jump to reach new heights, and bounce past deadly spikes. Collect every crystal in a level to open the exit - but watch out for violet. Once you collect it, the countdown begins!

## Controls

- **Move:** Arrow keys or A / D
- **Jump:** Space, ↑, or W
- **Double jump:** Press jump again while airborne
- **Wall jump:** Press jump while sliding against a wall
- **Mobile:** Use the on-screen direction and jump buttons

## Chase your best run

Your time and deaths are tracked throughout the game. Can you restore the rainbow faster and with fewer falls?
